- Original issuer PDFs and independent dated source records support 89 unique gross cash events. The new issuer parser recognizes both per-10-share and per-share stock bonus/capital transfers without confusing pre-tax with after-tax values.
- Of 89 original issuer cash events, 8 have source-positive share bonuses/capital transfers and 22 have full independently readable issuer stock rights.
